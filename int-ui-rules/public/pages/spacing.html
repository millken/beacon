Provenance and limits

Values come from tokens/inventory.json, collected from the source component trees.

These are observed component-tree values. They do not imply a universal spacing scale or a redesign.

Padding combines source paddingTop, paddingRight, paddingBottom and paddingLeft. Low values include source decoration; they are not all layout spacing recommendations.

0px stays visible

Zero-valued gaps and padding are retained as measured facts rather than removed from the inventory.
